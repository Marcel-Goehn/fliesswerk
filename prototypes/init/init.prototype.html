PROTOTYPE What does kraftwerk init ask, and what does it write?

Pick a sample project on the left. init first looks at the repo (no system scan) and pre-fills its answers, then asks you to confirm or change them. On the right you see what it prints and every file it would write. Change an answer and watch the files change. The tabs walk through the awkward cases: an unknown language, choosing no Sandbox, a teammate cloning an already set-up repo, and running init a second time.
